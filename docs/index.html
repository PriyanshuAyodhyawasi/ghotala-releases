Today

A list that doesn't let things slip.

Write what you need to do and tap it when it's done. Anything you don't finish moves to tomorrow. Hold ＋ and just say it.

Rhythm

Your routine, on a timeline.

Water, gym, study, skincare: the things you do every day, at their time. Tick them off and watch your week fill up.

Notes and Diary

Write like you're texting yourself.

Every note is a chat with times, photos, lists and voice notes. The diary is the same: say how you feel, write a line, look back later.

Ghotala AI

Bring your own AI. Then just talk.

Connect your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini key in Settings. Hold ＋ and say "Remind me to call mom at 6" or "Add gym to my routine". It asks what it needs, shows you what it will add, and adds it when you say yes.

🎙️

Talk, don't type

It listens, answers out loud, and keeps the conversation going. Hindi and Hinglish work too.

✅

You stay in charge

Nothing is added until you tap Add or say yes.

🔑

Your key, your AI

Your key stays encrypted on your phone. Ghotala has no servers, so nothing comes to us.

Install

Ready in a minute.

  1. Download Ghotala.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installing from your browser or Files app.
  3. Open Ghotala. A short demo shows you everything.

New versions install from inside the app with "Update now", and your data is kept. Prefer automatic updates? Add it to Obtainium. Works on Android 8.0 and newer.

No ads, no tracking, no account. Your data lives on your phone, and the optional backup goes to your own Google Drive.
