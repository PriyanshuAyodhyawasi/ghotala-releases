Today

A list for each day. Type “Call mom at 6pm” and the time sets itself. Unfinished items move to tomorrow.

🌿

Rhythm

Your routine on a timeline. Add a block in one line: “Water every 2 hours from 8am to 10pm”.

📖

Diary

Mood, feelings, a gentle prompt. Your week shows your mood, your Day Score and what makes your good days good.

🔒

Lock In

Focus with a live scene, lo-fi, rain or Hz tones, your songs, or Spotify and YouTube Music.

📝

Notes

Text, lists, photos, drawings and voice. Long-press a line to turn it into a reminder.

🔐

Vault

Passwords and secure notes, encrypted on your phone. Pull down on Today to open it.

🔔

Smart reminders

Snooze to tonight or tomorrow, 50 sounds to pick from, and keep-reminding tasks that won't let you slack.

🧩

Widgets

Today, Rhythm (tick blocks off right there) and a round Lock In timer.

Your backup

Install in a minute

  1. Download Ghotala.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installing from your browser or Files app.
  3. Open Ghotala. A five-tip tour shows you around.

Updates: when a new version is out, Ghotala shows “Update now” and installs it for you. Your data is kept. Prefer automatic updates? Add it to Obtainium.

Works on Android 8.0 and newer.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no account. Everything is stored on your phone; Google Drive backup is optional and goes to your own Drive. The Vault is encrypted with your PIN. Read the privacy policy.
