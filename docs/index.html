Ghotala

Commit. Remind. Act. Complete.

A personal accountability app for Android

Ghotala keeps you honest about what you said you'd do. Tasks don't sit silently in a list: Ghotala checks in with escalating reminders until you actually finish them.

Download for Android Privacy policy

What it does

  • Daily to-do list. A simple list for each day. Anything you don't finish moves to the next day automatically, so nothing gets lost.
  • Deadline tasks that nag. Set a deadline and a reminder interval; notifications become more urgent as the deadline approaches.
  • Plain-English quick add. Type "Call mom at 6pm" or "Finish report tomorrow at 5 PM".
  • Focus Mode, score and streaks. A countdown to your deadline and an accountability score that rewards finishing on time.
  • Home-screen widget showing today's list with tap-to-check items.
  • Private by default. Everything is stored on your phone. Optionally, you can back up to your own Google Drive.
