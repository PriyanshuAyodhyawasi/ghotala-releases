Privacy Policy

Ghotala for Android · Last updated October 16, 2026

In short

Ghotala has no servers and no accounts. Your tasks and lists live on your phone. If you choose to connect Google Drive, backups go to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

Data stored on your device

Ghotala stores the tasks, daily list items, reminder times, completion history and settings you create in the app's private storage on your phone. This data is used only to show your lists, schedule reminders and calculate your score inside the app.

If Android's built-in backup is enabled on your phone, Android may copy this app data to your Google account as part of your device backup. That backup is managed by Android and Google under your Google account settings.

Optional Google Drive backup

You can choose to connect your Google account to back up Ghotala's data. If you do:

  • What Ghotala accesses: only files Ghotala itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission), plus its own hidden application data folder (drive.appdata), where backups made by older versions were kept. Ghotala cannot see, read, change or delete any of your other Drive files, photos or documents.
  • What it stores there: a "Ghotala" folder you can open in Drive at any time, even without the app: your daily list and tasks as readable files, each note as a file with its photos, drawings and voice notes next to it, your Vault (still encrypted), and full backups in "Ghotala/Backups" for restoring.
  • How it's used: solely to save backups when you tap "Back up now" or when you turn on automatic backup, and to restore them when you choose "Restore from Drive".
  • Sharing: backups are transferred directly between your phone and your Google Drive. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party, and are not used for advertising, analytics or AI training.
  • Your control: tap "Disconnect" in Ghotala to stop using Drive. You can delete Ghotala's backups and revoke access at any time in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ghotala's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Password Vault

Logins, bank details, PINs and secure notes you save in the Vault are encrypted on your phone with AES-256. The key is derived from your Vault PIN and protected by your phone's hardware key store; fingerprint unlock uses a key that Android only releases after a successful fingerprint check. Your PIN and recovery code are never stored or sent anywhere. If the Vault is included in a backup (JSON or your own Google Drive), it stays encrypted and can only be opened with your PIN on the same phone or with your recovery code. Screenshots are blocked while the Vault is open.

What Ghotala does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts or location.
  • Photos, the camera and the microphone are used only when you add a photo, drawing or voice clip to a note, and those files stay in Ghotala's private storage on your phone.

Permissions on your phone

Ghotala asks for notification, exact-alarm and battery-optimization permissions only so that reminders fire on time. It uses the internet only to:

  • back up to and restore from your Google Drive, if you connect it;
  • stream Lock In music from Ghotala's public GitHub page (tracks are cached on your phone);
  • check GitHub for a newer version of Ghotala, at most every few hours.

These requests to GitHub send no personal data. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ghotala removes all data on your phone. Drive backups can be deleted from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Ghotala → Delete hidden app data).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/PriyanshuAyodhyawasi/ghotala-releases/issues.
